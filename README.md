# AREWA-HUB

## Northern Nigeria Marketplace Platform

AREWA HUB is a digital marketplace platform designed to connect buyers, sellers, and service providers across Northern Nigeria.

### Main Features

- Buyers can discover and purchase products.
- Sellers can list and sell products.
- Service providers can offer their services.
- Secure payment and transaction protection.
- Order and delivery management.
- Communication between buyers, sellers, and service providers.
- User accounts and profiles.
- Admin management and platform security.

### Vision

To build a trusted digital marketplace that makes buying, selling, and accessing services easier across Northern Nigeria.

**AREWA HUB — Connecting Buyers, Sellers & Services.**
